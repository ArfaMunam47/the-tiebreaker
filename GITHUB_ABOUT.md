## Full Description (for GitHub repo about section)

The Tiebreaker is a sophisticated AI-powered decision intelligence platform designed to help users make better choices through systematic, evidence-based analysis. 

**What it does:**
- Evaluates multiple options using weighted decision matrices
- Generates AI-powered clarifying questions to gather critical context
- Provides comprehensive analysis including SWOT, risk assessment, scenario modeling, and sensitivity analysis
- Identifies cognitive biases, assumptions, and blindspots
- Offers follow-up exploration through Think Deeper Chat
- Tracks decision outcomes and lessons learned over time

**Core Philosophy:** "Don't decide for me. Help me decide better."

The platform maintains strict decision integrity—user options are never silently modified, AI suggestions are clearly flagged, and the AI assists without making the final call. Every insight is classified as FACT, ASSUMPTION, INTERPRETATION, or UNKNOWN to ensure transparency.

**Tech Stack:**
- Frontend: React 19, TypeScript 5.8, Tailwind CSS 4.1, Vite 6
- Backend: Express.js 4.21, Google Gemini AI (gemini-3.6-flash)
- Features: Multi-user authentication, persistent decision library, export/import, version history

Perfect for career decisions, financial planning, business strategy, and any complex choice where structured thinking leads to better outcomes.
